# Chrome Web Store listing

## Name

LinkedIn Lens

## Short description

Categorize your LinkedIn feed with AI, mask the categories you choose, and reveal any post whenever you want.

## Detailed description

Open LinkedIn for updates in your field. Use LinkedIn Lens to help filter the noise.

Lens categorizes posts and lets you choose which categories to mask, including ads and promotions. Each masked post shows why it was hidden. Reveal it whenever you want, or correct a category yourself.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Choose from 60 editable categories across three levels, including technology, careers, research, news and promotions.
• Add your own categories, change their descriptions and colors, and choose which ones to mask.
• See category estimates and the active masking reason on each post.
• Keep scrolling while Lens analyzes visible posts and the next 10 posts already loaded by LinkedIn.
• Hide non-post cards such as job recommendation carousels separately from unreadable posts.
• Optionally generate a comment draft, edit it and insert it yourself. Lens never submits comments for you.

BRING YOUR OWN OPENROUTER KEY

AI processing requires your own OpenRouter account, API key and available credits. Provider charges may apply. Classification uses an AI decision model; optional comment drafts use a separately configurable text-generation model. The extension is not an offline classifier.

PRIVACY AND CONTROL

Cloud AI processing starts only after you explicitly consent and save your key. Post text, category definitions and post signals are sent to OpenRouter and its AI providers. When readable text is missing, the first attached image may be sent instead. Comment drafting also sends your writing direction when you click Generate.

Your key and preferences stay in extension-local storage. There is no developer analytics service. Lens does not read direct messages or deliberately collect comment threads, and does not automatically scroll, like posts, send messages or post comments.

Pause Lens, withdraw cloud consent, reveal individual posts or forget your key whenever you want. AI estimates can be wrong and provider response times vary. The request limit counts attempts, not dollars.

Independently developed. Not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn.
